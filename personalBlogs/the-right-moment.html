The Right Moment

We never truly miss the moment itself—we miss who we were when we lived it. The right time to live is now.

By Johnson Elangbam • October 5, 2026

Sometimes, we close our eyes and return to a beautiful moment from the past.

We remember how it felt—the laughter, the people, the warmth of those days—and we imagine that if we could somehow go back, even for a little while, we would finally feel that happiness again.

But when we do return, something feels strangely different.

The place may still be there.
The faces may still be familiar.
The memories may still live inside us.

Yet the feeling is gone.

And suddenly, what we thought would be a reunion with happiness becomes melancholy. Because we realize that we never truly miss the moment itself—we miss who we were when we lived it.

Time has moved quietly through everything. People have changed. Places have changed. And so have we.

The past is beautiful precisely because it cannot be lived again.

So don't spend the present longing for a moment that has already become a memory. One day, you may look back at this very moment with the same ache in your heart, wishing you had stayed a little longer, laughed a little louder, loved a little deeper, and noticed how beautiful it was while it was still happening.

The right time to live is not yesterday.
It is not tomorrow.
It is now.

Be present enough to recognize the moment you are living—because someday, this moment too will become the past you wish you could return to.
